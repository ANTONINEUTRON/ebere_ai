Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 8, 2026

At Ebere, a service by Tita Labs ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you chat with Ebere on Telegram, WhatsApp, or other platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly through your chats with Ebere. This includes:

  • Chat Messages & Media: Text, images, voice notes, or receipts you upload.
  • Platform Identifiers: Your unique Telegram user ID, WhatsApp phone number, and display names to maintain your session history.
  • Structured Logs: Transactions, reminders, tasks, and contact listings you save.

2. How We Process Your Data

To provide AI agent functionalities, Ebere uses large language models, including Google Gemini. Your text inputs are sent securely to these API endpoints to generate responses. We do not sell or lease your personal conversations or personal information to third parties.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We store your chat history and memory logs securely using MongoDB to allow Ebere to remember you across messaging sessions. If you wish to delete your data or reset your chat history, you can simply type "reset profile" or "delete my data" in the chat, or email support@titalabs.com.

4. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, please remember that no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Cookies and Web Analytics

This landing page does not store tracking cookies or track you across the web. We may monitor basic page hit counts to analyze landing page traffic, which is stored in an anonymized format.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@titalabs.com.
