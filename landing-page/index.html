System Capabilities

What you say, and how Ebere organizes it on the backend.

Bookkeeping

Speak or type plain income and expenses. Ebere extracts numbers, currencies, categories, and logs them in a structured ledger.

User says
"Just made ₦180k from a curtain job. Materials were ₦35k."

Needs & Offers

Post opportunities or standing requests. Ebere parses requirements and maps them to available local providers in real-time.

User says
"Find me a plumber in Ikeja" / "I'm a plumber available weekdays"

Housing Discovery

List properties or set housing search preferences. Ebere handles media matching, linking image uploads to listings.

User says
*sends photo* + "2-bedroom apartment available, Lekki"

Reminders & Schedules

Set timers or schedule recurring lookups. Ebere executes tasks in the background and delivers reports back when due.

User says
"Remind me to invoice the client tomorrow at 9am"
