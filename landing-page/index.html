Capabilities

Everything You Need, In One Chat

From tracking your money to finding local services — tell Ebere what you need in plain language.

Bookkeeping

Say your income and expenses naturally. Ebere extracts amounts, categories, and logs them in a structured ledger automatically.

You say
"Just made ₦180k from a curtain job. Materials were ₦35k."

Community Marketplace

Post services or find help nearby. Ebere proactively matches needs with offers and connects both parties directly.

You say
"Find me a plumber in Ikeja" / "I'm a plumber in Surulere"

Inventory Tracking

Track stock levels, cost prices, and selling prices. Ebere adjusts counts automatically when you buy or sell.

You say
"I have 100 bags of rice at ₦15k cost, ₦18k sell price"

Reminders & Schedules

Set one-off reminders or recurring tasks. Ebere delivers them to your chat on time, every time.

You say
"Remind me to invoice the client tomorrow at 9am"

Web Search

Ask any question and get real-time answers from the web, complete with cited sources — right in your chat.

You say
"What's the current dollar rate in Nigeria?"

Teach New Skills

Extend Ebere's abilities by saving custom skills in plain English. Every user's Ebere agent becomes uniquely theirs.
